What we look for

Something you finished

A robot, a tool, a dataset, an instrument. Finished beats ambitious.

Working mathematics

Linear algebra, probability, and calculus you can use, not just recognize.

Code you can defend

Python or C++ fluency, with version control habits.

Straight answers

Apply to the group
Do I need prior robotics experience?

No. About half of our projects start with strong software skills and a willingness to learn the hardware side. New students are paired with someone experienced.

Is this paid?

Many undergraduate positions carry a stipend or course credit. Funded graduate positions include tuition remission and a stipend.

Can I work part time during the semester?

Yes. Undergraduates typically work 8 to 12 hours per week during term, with the option of full-time summer work.

Do you take students from other departments?

Yes. Students in mathematics, physics, and electrical engineering regularly contribute to perception and control projects.
